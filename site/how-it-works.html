Six steps, mostly automatic

Choose your footage

Pick the input folder. Clips are discovered recursively and sorted by true shooting time — embedded metadata first, then the timestamp cameras put in the filename, and only then the file date. That order matters: copying footage off a card resets file dates, so the filename is what keeps a mixed-camera timeline honest.

Analysis & AI scoring

Every candidate scene is scored for sharpness, exposure, contrast, motion, stability and audio, then the offline neural network rates subject clarity across three sampled frames.

Selection

The best-scoring, most diverse moments are chosen. Junk is dropped, duplicates clustered, journey coverage guarantees every video appears, and cuts snap to speech pauses.

Review (optional)

With timeline review on, the app pauses and shows every clip as a card. Preview, trim In/Out with I/O keys, remove with Del, confirm with Enter.

Music & polish

Auto music lands on scenic sections ducked under speech, voice is denoised and normalized, transitions are applied, and an animated overlay is optional.

Render & verify

Segments render in parallel (GPU when available), assemble with crossfades, and the final file is verified for duration, audio presence and A/V sync before the app calls it done.
