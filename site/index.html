New in v0.7.0

Meera explains herself, and finds the moment you mean

🧠

Selection that ranks appeal New

An on-device CLIP model judges whether a shot is actually good, blended with people in frame, motion, speech and stand-out sounds. Every kept clip explains itself, and a skipped clip names the rival that beat it with the numbers side by side — "Why not this one?"

🔍

Search your footage New

Describe a moment — "the bit where we're eating by the sea" — and get timecodes back, from the same offline analysis pass. Meera also names what is in each clip (dog, food, car…) as filter chips, and "Or just describe it" turns a plain sentence into settings.

🎤

Talk mode and two cameras New

A lecture, interview or wedding speech is ranked by what was said. Two cameras from one event are lined up by what both microphones heard, and a wrong camera clock is corrected only on audio proof. Cuts start and finish on a full sentence.

🎵

Reels, karaoke captions, your language New

A strict 60-second best-of Reel with face-anchored portrait reframe, cut to music Meera generates herself. Karaoke captions light each word as it is spoken; an optional multilingual model captions Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Urdu, Spanish, French and more, with translate-to-English.

Also in 0.7.0: one decode pass per video (much faster), working GPU encoding, 4K export, queue and watch folders, saved projects, resume and undo. Everything new is off by default. Full changelog ➤
