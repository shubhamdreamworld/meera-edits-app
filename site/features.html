Feature tour

Everything the first cut needs.
Nothing it doesn't.

19 capabilities, all running locally on your Windows PC.

Smart selection
🧠

Offline AI shot selection

A bundled MobileNetV2 neural network (ONNX) samples three frames per scene and scores subject clarity, focus confidence and visual salience — blended with classic sharpness, exposure, contrast, motion and stability metrics. Your footage never leaves the machine.

🗑

Skips repetitive footage New

Fifty takes of the same rafting run? Near-identical whole videos are grouped as one scene and only the best few are used — the strongest moments are pulled from those instead of grinding through every file. Genuine standouts still get through.

🔄

AI scene-similarity dedup

Two shots of the same subject from a different angle produce similar AI embeddings but different pixel hashes. Cosine similarity between embeddings catches them, so the same scene never repeats itself across your edit.

🗣

Speech-safe cuts

Silence detection snaps every cut to a natural pause. If no reliable pause exists in the window, the original boundary is kept — a sentence is never sliced in half.

🗺

Journey coverage

Long trip? Every source video is guaranteed at least one moment in the final cut, so no location silently disappears from your story.

🔁

Instant re-runs

Visual, audio and AI scores plus rendered segments are cached per source file. Tweak a setting and re-export in a fraction of the original time.

Footage handling
🕑

True shooting-time order New

Clips are placed in the order they were actually shot, across mixed cameras. Copying footage off a card resets its file date, so Meera Edits also reads the timestamp cameras embed in the filename (DJI, IMG_, VID_, PXL_ and more), which survives copying. The log shows which time source each clip used.

🔄

Upside-down correction New

Footage shot with the camera held the wrong way up carries no rotation metadata, so nothing could fix it. The built-in model checks whether a clip reads better flipped and turns it upright at render — staying deliberately cautious, leaving shots with no clear "up" (water, sky) untouched.

🗑

Junk rejection

Very dark, blurry or shaky footage is filtered out before it ever reaches your timeline.

📺

Mixed orientation handling

Portrait, landscape and square clips blend cleanly — the blurred-background fill keeps every frame full without ugly black bars.

Output & style
🎬

One run, five formats

A single analysis pass renders a YouTube 16:9 master, a 9:16 Shorts cut capped at 60 seconds, a 1:1 square promo, a ~45-second motion-weighted teaser and a speech-free b-roll compilation.

✨

Sharper output New

A light denoise and gentle sharpen pass is applied to every rendered clip, so the finished video looks cleaner than the raw footage. Automatic in Balanced and Quality modes, skipped in Turbo for speed.

🖌️

World-class text overlays

Five polished styles — minimal, elegant, bold, lower-third and boxed — with your choice of font, accent colour, position and fade or slide animation. Text outlines give broadcast-grade legibility on any footage.

🎵

Background music, your way

Choose None, Smart (music only over quiet, scenic or recap sections) or Whole video. Original peaceful music fades in and out and is side-chain ducked under every voice.

Control & reliability
✂️

Visual timeline review

Pause before render: every chosen clip becomes a card with scores and status. Open the trim studio to preview (any codec — HEVC included), set In/Out points, or drop clips entirely, with full keyboard control.

📋

Run & crash log

Every edit writes a timestamped log next to your footage. Errors and even hard crashes are recorded, so an overnight batch never fails silently — you always know exactly what happened.

🌑

Overnight durability

Corrupt files are skipped, transient locks retried, hung processes killed by watchdogs, and every rendered segment is checkpointed — a crash at 3am resumes where it stopped.

⚡

GPU accelerated

NVIDIA NVENC, Intel Quick Sync and AMD AMF encoders are auto-detected with a clean CPU fallback, and encoder choice is logged for every render.

🔒

Private by design

No cloud APIs, no telemetry, no account, no installation key. Completely free to use.
