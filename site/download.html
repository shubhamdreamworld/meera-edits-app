🛡️ Seeing "Windows protected your PC"?

That blue screen appears for every brand-new app that hasn't yet built up download reputation with Microsoft — it doesn't mean anything is wrong with Meera Edits. To continue:

  1. Click More info on the blue dialog.
  2. Click the Run anyway button that appears.
  3. The installer opens normally — follow it through.

The warning fades over time as more people install the app. If your browser also warns while downloading the .exe, choose Keep.

System requirements

ComponentMinimum
OSWindows 10 or 11, 64-bit
RAM8 GB (16 GB recommended for 4K footage)
GPUOptional — NVIDIA / Intel / AMD used automatically when present
DiskRoughly 2× your selected footage size free for temporary renders
InternetNot required — analysis, AI and rendering are fully offline. One opt-in feature, "internet music", downloads a public-domain background track from archive.org when you turn it on; it sends only a fixed search for ambient public-domain music, never your footage.
